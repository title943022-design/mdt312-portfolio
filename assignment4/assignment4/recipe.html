Recipe: Pizza Dip

Pull-apart skillet dip with seasoned dough, marinara, and melted mozzarella

Ingredients

- FOR THE PULL-APART BREAD

  • 16.3 ounce can Grands biscuit dough, 8 count
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 teaspoons parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

- FOR THE PIZZA DIP

  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 teaspoons Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara sauce, or pizza sauce
  • 1 ½ cups mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 8 slices pepperoni, quartered (optional topping)

Instructions

Step 1: Preheat the oven to 350°F.

Step 2: Cut each biscuit in half, then roll each half of dough into a ball.

Step 3: Combine melted butter, parmesan cheese, and 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning in a bowl. Roll dough in seasoned butter.

Step 4: Arrange them around the edge of a greased large cast iron skillet.

Step 5 & 6: Beat cream cheese, ricotta cheese, and 2 teaspoons seasoning. Spread mixture into the center of the skillet.

Step 7 (Part 1): Top with marinara sauce.

Step 7 (Part 2): Top with shredded mozzarella and remaining Italian seasoning.

Step 8: Top with pepperonis. Place in the oven and bake for 25–30 minutes.
