Recipe: Cinnamon Roll Wreath with Apples

Ingredients you’ll need

  • Crescent roll dough: You can use canned crescent roll dough or sheets of canned crescent dough.
  • Granulated sugar
  • Brown sugar
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Unsalted butter: Softened prior to making the recipe (room temperature).
  • Pazazz apples: Sweet, tart, and extra crispy apples.
  • cheese
  • Milk
  • Vanilla extract
  • Powdered sugar

Step by step instructions

  1. Roll out the crescent roll dough: Place a large piece of parchment paper on a flat work surface. Place both sheets slightly overlapping and press seams together.
  2. Top with the cinnamon sugar butter: Combine softened butter with granulated sugar, brown sugar, and ground cinnamon. Spread evenly across the dough.
  3. Add the apples: Core and thinly slice one Pazazz apple, then place slices on top in an even layer (not overlapping).
  4. Roll it up: Roll the crescent roll dough up tightly over the apples and cinnamon sugar butter into a tube.
  5. Slice it in half: Use a sharp knife to slice lengthwise through the middle of the tube.
  6. Separate the two halves: Once you’ve sliced the tube, separate the two halves and ensure the cinnamon butter and apples are facing up.
  7. Braid them together: Carefully braid the two halves together, lifting one half over the other.
  8. Form the wreath: Bring the two ends together to form a wreath. Bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown.
